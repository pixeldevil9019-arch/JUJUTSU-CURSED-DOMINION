# Arena prototype controls

- **Move:** touch and drag on the left half of the landscape screen. The initial touch position acts as the joystick center.
- **Cursed Strike:** tap on the right half in the lower portion of the screen.
- **Range:** approach a target; the strike only affects nearby enemies.
- **Cooldown:** strikes have a short cooldown, shown by the small meter under the health bar.
- **Survival:** enemy contact reduces player health. At zero health, the prototype respawns the player and resets the current encounter.
- **Waves:** clear the active group to spawn the next wave. Enemy durability increases as the wave number rises.

This is a touch-input prototype. It currently uses geometric placeholder visuals and simple enemy pursuit rather than final animation, effects or 3D assets.
